The Problem

Work Shouldn't Get Stuck in Broken Promises

Knowledge workers make commitments daily across Slack, meetings, emails, and PRs. Most disappear until someone gets angry.

1. The 60% Promise Drop-off
"I'll send the SOC2 checklist by Friday" or "I'll review the architecture PR tomorrow" slip through the cracks because informal commitments are never structured or continuously monitored.
Status: Untracked & Forgotten
2. The Human Nagging Tax
Managers and leads spend 20%+ of their workweek checking in, sending Slack DMs, and scheduling status meetings just to ask: "Is this done yet?"
Cost: 4–6 hrs/week wasted per lead
3. The Static Issue-Tracker Flaw
Jira and Linear record intent through manual ticket creation, but they provide zero autonomous follow-through, zero objective evidence inspection, and zero proactive risk intervention.
Gap: Passive record, not active agent
The Solution

FollowFlow: The Autonomous AI Employee

Instead of another app you have to manage, FollowFlow works autonomously in the background and only surfaces when a human decision is needed.

✦ Zero-Overhead AI Extraction
The inline Strands AI Copilot extracts structured promises directly from conversation: owner (@username), deliverable criteria, deadline horizon, and team SLA scope.
⚙ Autonomous 5-Step Agent Loop
Continuously runs Observe → Reason → Act → Wait → Verify. Calculates deadline horizons, monitors risk tiers (Active, Due Soon, Critical), and dispatches contextual follow-ups via Amazon SES.
🔍 Objective Multi-App Verification
Validates completion against real artifacts: merged GitHub PRs, Slack #Done receipts, Notion PRD statuses, and AWS S3 deliverable uploads.
🛡 Ambiguity Stop Rule (HITL Gateway)
When confidence is high, it marks work fulfilled autonomously. When evidence is ambiguous or conflicting, it pauses, explains why it stopped, and awaits a 1-click human decision.
